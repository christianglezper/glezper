Glezper 360°

Take your brand from where it is to where you want it to be.

Communication with direction.

Glezper 360° is our integrated communications practice, helping businesses and organizations turn ideas, challenges and objectives into clear, intentional communication.

From the words your audience reads to the strategy behind them, we build communications around what you need to accomplish—not around a predetermined package.

Strategic Communications

Communication begins with knowing what needs to be said, who needs to hear it and what should happen next.

We develop communications strategies that connect organizational goals with audiences, messages, channels and measurable objectives.

Public Relations

Reputation is built long before anyone needs a press release.

We help organizations develop their public presence through media relations, corporate communications, stakeholder engagement, positioning and communications planning.

Brand Messaging & Copywriting

The right message should sound unmistakably like your organization.

From brand voice and campaign concepts to websites, advertising copy, presentations and corporate materials, we translate strategy into language people can understand—and remember.

Digital Presence

Your website and digital channels are often where audiences decide whether to keep paying attention.

We develop websites, landing pages, content and digital communications designed around the customer journey and the actions you want audiences to take.

Media Budget Allocation

Make the budget work before you buy the media.

We evaluate your objectives, audiences, market and available budget to determine how your media investment should be distributed across the channels that make the most sense for your organization.

Rather than beginning with inventory to sell, we begin with what you need to accomplish—then build the allocation around it.

Crisis Communications

When circumstances change quickly, communication needs structure.

We help organizations prepare for and navigate difficult situations through crisis planning, messaging, stakeholder communications and response strategy.

Internal & Stakeholder Communications

Your employees, partners and other stakeholders are audiences too.

We help organizations communicate change, priorities and important information clearly across the people who make the organization work.

Have something to communicate?

Let's figure out the clearest way to say it—and the smartest way to make it matter.
