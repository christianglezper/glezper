## **Ideas need infrastructure.**

Glezper Business brings together practical solutions designed to help businesses operate, grow and pursue new opportunities.

### Business Solutions

**Built around what the business needs next.**

Every company reaches points where growth requires something beyond a good idea: capital, technology, better processes, new customers or the right strategic partner.

Glezper Business works at that intersection, helping businesses identify opportunities and access solutions that can move them forward.

### Access to Commercial Capital

**Opportunity needs resources.**

For qualifying businesses seeking additional working capital, Glezper Business provides access to commercial financing opportunities through established funding partners.

Funding may be used for needs such as inventory, equipment, expansion, marketing, renovations or other business expenses.

Eligibility, available products, amounts and terms are determined by the applicable funding provider and the circumstances of each business.

Start with a short inquiry about your business, monthly revenue and funding needs. We review each inquiry before discussing possible next steps. Businesses at different revenue levels are welcome to inquire; available options depend on the provider and your business profile.

### Payment Processing & POS

**Make it easier to get paid.**

Opening a business, adding online payments or reviewing your current processing costs? Tell us how you sell, what you use today and what you would like to improve. We can assess your needs and explore suitable provider options with you.

### Process Automation

**Give repetitive work a better workflow.**

Lead follow-up, appointment reminders, invoicing, customer service and reporting can take time away from running your business. Tell us which processes are slowing you down and which tools you already use. We will review the opportunity and discuss whether a specialist partner could help.

### Growth & Commercial Strategy

**From opportunity to execution.**

Sometimes the missing piece isn't another product or platform. It's knowing what to do next.

Working alongside the communications capabilities of Glezper 360° and the audience and advertising capabilities of Glezper Media, Glezper Business can help identify commercial opportunities and build a practical path toward pursuing them.

### The Glezper Advantage

**Business doesn't happen in silos.**

A company may need capital to launch an initiative, technology to manage the resulting leads and communications to bring the offer to market.

Because Glezper brings those disciplines together, we can look at the larger business objective instead of treating every problem as a separate transaction.
